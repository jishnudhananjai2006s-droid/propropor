Startline privacy

Startline is for people aged 18 and over.

What we keep

An account id (from Google, or a one-way hash of your email, never the email itself), your subscription status, and a backup of your tasks, races and progress so it follows you between devices. We do not keep your name, email address or birth date.

AI planning

When you ask for a plan, your goal, your answers and the dates are sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, Groq or Anthropic) to write the plan. When you ask for a study method, the task name is sent. Nothing is sent unless you tap the button.

Focus lock (Android)

If you turn it on, Startline uses Usage access to see which app is in front, and Display over other apps to show a "back to your task" screen. This happens only while your own focus timer runs, only on your phone, and nothing about the apps you use is stored or sent anywhere. You choose which apps are paused and can turn the lock off at any time.

Delete your data

In the app, open Report, then Settings and account, and choose Erase or Delete account.
